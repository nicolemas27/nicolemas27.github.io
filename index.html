About

Hi, I'm Nicole

I build things to understand them — fraud detection systems, RAG pipelines, adaptive learning platforms, clinical prediction models.

Multimodal Learning for Disease Risk Prediction
MIMIC-III is a de-identified ICU database from Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center in Boston — 58,000 admissions, over a decade of critical care records. My dissertation builds a model that reads all four data types together: patient demographics, timestamped vitals, clinical events, and free-text doctor notes. LSTM and Transformer encoders handle each stream, connected by cross-attention, then jointly predict mortality, length of stay, and clinical phenotypes in one pass.
