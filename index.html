About

Hi, I'm Nicole!

Data is everywhere, but raw data on its own means nothing.

What interests me is the process of making sense of it: exploring it, finding the patterns hidden inside, and building something useful from what you find.

My dissertation fuses patient demographics, timestamped vitals, clinical events, and clinical notes from the MIMIC-III ICU database to jointly predict mortality, length of stay, and clinical phenotypes. ICU data is noisy, incomplete, and multimodal, and that complexity is precisely what the architecture is designed to handle.

Before Manchester I completed a First Class BEng in AI & Data Science in Mumbai, which gave me the foundation to build on.

Dissertation 2026 — ongoing
Multimodal Learning for Disease Risk Prediction
MIMIC-III is a de-identified ICU database from Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center in Boston, 58,000 admissions, over a decade of critical care records.

My dissertation builds a model that reads all four data types together: patient demographics, timestamped vitals, clinical events, and free-text doctor notes. LSTM and Transformer encoders handle each stream, connected by cross-attention, then jointly predict mortality, length of stay, and clinical phenotypes in one pass.
In progressArchitecture designed to jointly predict 3 clinical outcomes — mortality, length of stay, and phenotypes — not yet finalised or evaluated.

AI-Enhanced Learning Platform — LearnTube
Turns any YouTube video into a personalised study session — quizzes, flashcards, summaries, and a concept map — driven by three adaptive models running in parallel per learner.

Bayesian Knowledge Tracing treats mastery as a hidden variable and updates it after every answer. A 3-parameter IRT engine selects each next question by maximising Fisher information at the learner's estimated ability. Three competing forgetting-curve models — Ebbinghaus, ACT-R, and a personalised BayesianRidge — race to predict the optimal review date, scored on held-out MAE/RMSE. Teaching-strategy experiments run through a proper A/B pipeline: power analysis before launch, Holm–Bonferroni correction, and Cohen's d effect sizes.
29unit tests covering the adaptive engine, plus a Chrome extension for in-browser quizzing
